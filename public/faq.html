Frequently asked questions

How long does a custom order take?

Most custom pieces are finished within 1–3 weeks depending on how many orders are ahead of yours. You'll hear back by email once yours is underway. Pet-chewable pieces can take a bit longer than that — see below.

Why do pet-chewable pieces take longer?

Chewable pieces have to be made from specific safe woods, fully untreated and food-grade finished, with extra care taken at every step — an improperly made chew piece is a real risk to a pet's health. We'd rather take the extra time to get it right than rush it, so build these a bit slower than our standard walking steeecks.

How is pricing determined?

Every piece is priced individually based on the size/length of the wood, the time invested in shaping and finishing it, current wood availability, and shipping. Add-on accessories like a paracord wrap, leather wrap, or other extras cost a bit more on top of the base price. We'll always confirm the exact total by email before you pay.

How much is shipping?

Shipping cost depends on the length of the piece and your location — long items like staffs cost more to ship than a poker or pet-size steeeck. We'll confirm the exact cost before you pay.

What's the refund policy?

Because every piece is made to order, payments are only accepted after your order details are confirmed by email. Payments sent before that confirmation are not eligible for a refund.

Can I pick the exact wood?

Yes — let us know hardwood or pine preference in your order, and we'll pick a piece that matches the look and strength you're after.

Can I customize every detail?

Yes — the order form has a quick basic version (size, wood, notes), plus an optional "customize every detail" section you can expand for bark placement, natural bends, wood burning, grip wraps, and more. Use as much or as little of it as you want.

How will I know when my order ships?

We'll email you tracking information as soon as it ships, so you always know exactly where it is.

What if my piece arrives damaged?

Contact us right away with a photo of the damage and we'll make it right — that's on us, not you.

Do you ship outside the US?

Reach out with your location before ordering and we'll confirm whether we can ship there and what it would cost.
