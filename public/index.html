Know Your Wood

Two woods, two jobs.

Not every steeeck is built for the same trail — here's how we choose the wood for yours.

Built to Last

Hardwood

Dense, heavy, and durable. This is what goes with you up the mountain — a hardwood staff will outlast years of real climbing and rough use.

Made to Show

Softwood — Pine

Our most-used wood. Lighter and easier to carve into detail, pine steeecks are the ones you display, gift, or carry for the look as much as the walk.

Get It Right

Sizing guide

Not sure what to order? Use these as a starting point — every steeeck can still be custom-cut to an exact length.

Who it's forTypical length
Adult (5'4"–6'0")48"–55"
Adult, taller (6'1"+)55"–60"

Rule of thumb for adults: a steeeck should reach about wrist height when your arm hangs relaxed at your side.

Keep It Trail-Ready

Caring for your steeeck

A little upkeep keeps the wood strong and the finish looking sharp for years.

Every Few Months

Oil the wood

A light coat of boiled linseed or mineral oil keeps the wood from drying out and cracking, especially after heavy use outdoors.

After Wet Trips

Dry it fully

If it gets rained on or dunked in a stream, let it air-dry fully before storing — trapped moisture is the main cause of splitting.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

How long does a custom order take?

Most custom pieces are finished within 1–3 weeks depending on how many orders are ahead of yours. You'll hear back by email once yours is underway.

How much is shipping?

Shipping cost depends on the length of the piece and your location — long items like staffs cost more to ship than a poker or pet-size steeeck. We'll confirm the exact cost before you pay.

What's the refund policy?

Because every piece is made to order, payments are only accepted after your order details are confirmed by email. Payments sent before that confirmation are not eligible for a refund.

Can I pick the exact wood?

Yes — let us know hardwood or pine preference in your order, and we'll pick a piece that matches the look and strength you're after.
