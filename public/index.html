Know Your Wood

Two woods, two jobs.

Not every stick is built for the same trail — here's how we choose the wood for yours.

Built to Last

Hardwood

Dense, heavy, and durable. This is what goes with you up the mountain — a hardwood staff will outlast years of real climbing and rough use.

Made to Show

Softwood — Pine

Our most-used wood. Lighter and easier to carve into detail, pine sticks are the ones you display, gift, or carry for the look as much as the walk.
