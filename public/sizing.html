Get It Right

Sizing guide

Not sure what to order? Use these as a starting point — every steeeck can still be custom-cut to an exact length.

Who it's forTypical length
Adult (5'4"–6'0")48"–55"
Adult, taller (6'1"+)55"–60"
Kids (ages 6–12)30"–40"
Baby's first steeeck (display, not for walking on)12"–18"
Pets (small–medium breeds)14"–22"
Fire poker28"–34"

Rule of thumb for adults: a steeeck should reach about wrist height when your arm hangs relaxed at your side.
