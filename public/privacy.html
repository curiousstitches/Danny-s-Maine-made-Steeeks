Privacy & Terms

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you create an account, we store your email address and the date you signed up. That's it — we don't collect payment details, addresses, or anything else through the sign-up form.

What we don't collect

We don't store your card, Cash App, or Venmo information. Payments happen directly on those apps' own platforms — this site never sees or touches your payment details.

Site visits

We log basic, anonymous visit counts (just a number, no personal info) so we know how many people are checking out the site.

Custom orders

Order details you email us (size, wood preference, what it's for) are used only to build and fulfill your order. We don't share them with anyone else.

Payments & refunds

Please email your order details before sending payment. Because pieces are handmade to order, payments sent without confirming order details first are not eligible for a refund. Once your order is confirmed and in progress, reach out directly if an issue comes up — we'll work with you.

Account deletion

Want your account and data removed? Email mainesteeecksupport@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

Questions

Reach out anytime at mainesteeecksupport@gmail.com.
