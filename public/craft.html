Know Your Wood

Two woods, two jobs.

Not every steeeck is built for the same trail — here's how we choose the wood for yours.

Built to Last

Hardwood

Dense, heavy, and durable. This is what goes with you up the mountain — a hardwood staff will outlast years of real climbing and rough use.

Made to Show

Softwood — Pine

Our most-used wood. Lighter and easier to carve into detail, pine steeecks are the ones you display, gift, or carry for the look as much as the walk.

Keep It Trail-Ready

Caring for your steeeck

A little upkeep keeps the wood strong and the finish looking sharp for years.

Every Few Months

Oil the wood

A light coat of boiled linseed or mineral oil keeps the wood from drying out and cracking, especially after heavy use outdoors.

After Wet Trips

Dry it fully

If it gets rained on or dunked in a stream, let it air-dry fully before storing — trapped moisture is the main cause of splitting.

Also Available

Canes for elderly & mobility support

Not every steeeck is for the trail — we also make canes built for everyday stability and comfort.

Custom Fit

Height-matched to you

A cane that's the wrong height can throw off balance instead of helping it. We custom-cut to your exact height for a proper, comfortable fit — not a one-size-fits-all length.

Grip & Stability

Ergonomic handles & non-slip tips

Choose a rounded, crook, or offset handle for a comfortable grip, paired with a rubber non-slip tip for secure footing on any surface.

If a cane is being used for a medical condition or balance issue, we'd recommend checking with a doctor or physical therapist on the right height and style for you before ordering — we're happy to build to whatever they recommend.
